About Vaheed

Full-stack independence, from design to deployment

I'm an independent Web Developer and Product Builder based in India. With a background in Computer Science, Mathematics, and Electronics (B.Sc.), I design and engineer fast, dependable digital products—from high-converting commercial websites to bespoke database-driven web applications.

I believe in clean, hand-crafted code without unnecessary dependencies or bloated frameworks. Every project is built from the ground up to load instantly, function reliably on mobile devices, and deliver measurable results for business owners.

When working with me, there are no handoffs or agency friction: you get a single point of accountability covering UI/UX design, front-end implementation, backend PHP/MySQL logic, hosting, and technical SEO.

About

I build the whole thing, start to finish

I'm an independent web developer based in India. I build business websites and web applications, with a focus on moving, packers and logistics companies — a niche where a clear phone-friendly site is often the difference between an enquiry and a missed call.

My background is a BSc in Maths, Electronics and Computer Science, and I have been building and shipping sites for small businesses since then. I work on the design, the front-end, the PHP behind the forms and the hosting setup, so there is one person to ask when something needs to change.

I write code by hand and keep things small on purpose: fewer moving parts means a site that is faster, cheaper to host and easier to fix later.

Currently learning: Python, automation.
